## 2. Our Story (About Page)

### The Woman Behind the Name

Mama Oliech Restaurant was founded by Mary Auma Oliech, known warmly across Nairobi as "Mama Oliech." She built her name on one simple promise: cook the fish the way it's meant to be cooked, with the traditional flavors of Western Kenya, and treat every guest — whether a neighbor or a head of state — like family.

Her food became a bridge between cultures, introducing the rich culinary traditions of the Lake Victoria region to a city, and eventually a world, hungry for something authentic.

### A Family Legacy, On and Off the Pitch

Mama Oliech's story is inseparable from her son, Dennis Oliech — one of Kenyan football's most celebrated strikers. Dennis earned 76 caps for the Harambee Stars and scored 34 international goals, a national record bettered only by William Ouma. He's remembered for the historic goal against Cape Verde that sent Kenya to the 2004 Africa Cup of Nations, and for a standout career in France's Ligue 1 with Auxerre, where he scored 25 goals in 165 appearances.

Dennis used his earnings from football to help grow his mother's restaurant, funding the flagship Hurlingham branch on Marcus Garvey Road and a second location paired with a pub at Dagoretti Corner. Football success, in a very real way, helped put Lake Victoria tilapia on Nairobi's map.

Since Mary Auma's passing in 2018, the restaurant has stayed in the family's hands, with her daughter Lilian now leading the flagship Hurlingham location — keeping her mother's recipes and her mother's warmth alive.

## Notes for Whoever Builds the Site

1. **Verify everything marked in brackets** — hours, addresses, delivery platform status, and prices change, and this draft intentionally avoids guessing at current details.
2. **Keep the story front and center.** The Mama Oliech / Dennis Oliech narrative and the celebrity visits are the strongest differentiators — lead with them on the homepage and About page rather than burying them.
3. **Don't publish exact prices on marketing pages** if they vary across delivery platforms — link to a single, easy-to-update menu source instead.
4. **Only list active branches.** Past/closed locations don't need to appear on the public site.
5. **Photos matter a lot here** — the fish, the greens, the wooden interior, the family — this is a visual, sensory brand. Prioritize good food photography over stock imagery.
